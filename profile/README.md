## `01` / What we build

LecoDevLabs Open Source is a home for open-source software, engineering experiments, developer tools and infrastructure projects.

We care about more than making things work. We care about **how they work, why they work, and whether they can be improved**.

Our approach combines practical development, technical curiosity and an engineering mindset. Projects evolve through implementation, testing, documentation, review and iteration.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>⚙️ Systems engineering</h3>

Low-level concepts, backend architecture, runtimes, infrastructure and the components behind reliable software.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>◈ AI & automation</h3>

Intelligent tools, agents, integrations and workflows that connect capabilities with real applications.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>⬡ Security & cryptography</h3>

Security-focused engineering, cryptographic technologies, careful implementation and measurable validation.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h3>⌘ Developer experience</h3>

Utilities, APIs, command-line tools and practical improvements that make development more productive.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## `04` / Engineering principles

<table>
<tr>
<td width="64" align="center" valign="top">

### 01

</td>
<td valign="top">

**Clarity over complexity**

Prefer understandable designs, explicit trade-offs and maintainable implementations.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="64" align="center" valign="top">

### 02

</td>
<td valign="top">

**Evidence over assumptions**

Test important behavior, validate changes and be honest about limitations.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="64" align="center" valign="top">

### 03

</td>
<td valign="top">

**Security by design**

Treat trust boundaries, dependencies, credentials and failure modes as engineering concerns.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="64" align="center" valign="top">

### 04

</td>
<td valign="top">

**Progress through iteration**

Build, measure, review, improve and document. A good engineering process is continuous.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="64" align="center" valign="top">

### 05

</td>
<td valign="top">

**Open by intention**

Share code, decisions and documentation whenever licensing, privacy and security permit it.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## `05` / Build with us

Open source is not just about publishing code. It is about creating something other people can understand, use, challenge and improve.

Whether you are a developer, tester, technical writer or curious learner, there are different ways to help.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**01 — Report**

Find a reproducible bug? Open an issue with the relevant details, expected behavior and steps to reproduce it.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**02 — Improve**

Suggest focused improvements, fix bugs, add tests or improve existing documentation.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**03 — Review**

Read the code, question assumptions and help make implementations more reliable.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**04 — Share**

Use a project according to its license and share constructive feedback with its maintainers.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
